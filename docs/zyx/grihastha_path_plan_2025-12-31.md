## Executive Summary

This document outlines a 21-year Grihastha (householder) path plan that integrates:

- **Primary Dharma**: Grain OS development as foundational duty
- **Values**: Veganism, animal care, MMT (Modern Monetary Theory), peace, creativity
- **Vision**: Beautiful large Grain collective collaborative space for creativity, peace, meaningful freedom, and love
- **Realistic Constraints**: Unlikelihood of direct MMT funding without credentials/track record
- **Intuition**: Focus on Grain OS first, avoid security/military/government complexity

**Core Framework**: Grain OS as primary duty (dharma), sustainable livelihood (artha) supporting Grain, creative community space (kama), and charity/service (dana).

---

## Vedic Ashrama Context

### Grihastha Stage (Ages 25-50)

**Primary Duties**:
1. **Dharma** (Righteous Duty): Grain OS development as foundational work
2. **Artha** (Prosperity): Sustainable livelihood that supports Grain development
3. **Kama** (Legitimate Desires): Creative space, community, love, meaningful freedom
4. **Dana** (Charity): Giving 1/10th of earnings to support others

**Key Grihastha Responsibilities**:
- Marriage and family (optional, if desired)
- Earning livelihood to support family with dignity
- Raising and educating children (if chosen)
- Performing religious rituals (Pancha Maha Yajnas)
- Contributing to community welfare through charity and social obligations
- Supporting other ashramas (students, renunciants)

**Pancha Maha Yajnas (Five Great Sacrifices)**:
1. **Bhuta Yajna**: Care for all beings (veganism, animal sanctuary work)
2. **Manushya Yajna**: Hospitality to guests (collaborative space, community)
3. **Pitri Yajna**: Ancestral offerings (honoring lineage, family)
4. **Deva Yajna**: Fire rituals, divine offerings (spiritual practice)
5. **Brahma Yajna**: Study and teaching of Vedas (Grain OS teaching, knowledge sharing)
