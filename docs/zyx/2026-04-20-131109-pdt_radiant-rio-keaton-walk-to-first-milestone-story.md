## The order of ideas (dependencies)

Call it **stairs**. Each tread holds your weight; the landing is the milestone.

**Step 1 — Name the smallest useful thing.**  
**One** offer: a week of help, a small app, a short report, a pilot ledger, one corridor story with numbers—something you can say in **two sentences** at a kitchen table. That size is the **unit of truth**.

**Step 2 — Name who it is for.**  
One face, one desk, one watershed: coop manager, fund associate, creator who wants sponsors **visible**, clerk who wants cleaner books, friend who pays you to **prove** the idea. **Someone** carries the story first; the **circle widens** from there.

**Step 3 — Ask for money in a shape that fits the work.**  
Invoice, retainer, subscription, or a check after a sample—**clear scope**, **clear price**, **clear calendar**. Trust with strangers starts when **both sides see the same box on the table**.

**Step 4 — Do the work in daylight.**  
Plain words each day: what moved, what stuck, what surprised you. Keep the hard parts; they are **map**. The next buyer pays for the **path** you already walked.

**Step 5 — Keep the promise in front; let tools speed the repeat.**  
Code, ledgers, phones are **servants** of the promise. They earn their place when they **cut time** on the next delivery. Lead with the **handshake**; automate what repeats.

**Step 6 — Hand back the story with receipts.**  
One page: what they asked, what you did, what changed, what it cost, what comes next. That page lets angels and partners **carry you** into the next room while you stay at the bench.

**Step 7 — Widen one ring.**  
Second buyer, same offer—or the **small** feature the first buyer asked for, still **one** spine, **one** truth in your ledger.

These seven are the **dependencies**. Honor the order; the climb **feels** solid when someone leans on the rail.

## How the big dreams ride with you

**Train and canola**, **veganic** fields, **Silken Ground** images of cable and soil, the **phone** that asks **gentler** things of the earth—they are **true north**. They lift your standards. **Rent arrives** when **Step 3** carries a real name on the invoice. On the way there, true north **steers** which gigs deserve your weeks.

Hold the **Silken Ground** questions close: does this week’s work **nourish**, **circulate**, **return what it takes**? When all three answer **yes**, widen the trail. When clarity **asks for rest**, **return to the stairs** with thanks and a lighter pack—**course correction** as care.

## A simple first milestone (pick one lane for ninety days)

Choose **for the next ninety days**; choose again later.

**Path A — Money from work.**  
One paid package. One price on a page. One yes. Deliver. Invoice. Repeat once.

**Path B — Money from product.**  
One small app or one paid tier people use weekly. Ship. Watch. **Tend** what wavers. Bill again next month.

**Path C — Money from trust.**  
One angel or small fund buys a **named** bundle—diligence room, transparency sprint, plain-English **risk map** of your stack (**work first**, per the services essay). The milestone is **cash plus a letter you can show**.

Any path can be **the** first milestone. Pick **one**; let the others breathe beside the trail.
