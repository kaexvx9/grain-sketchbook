### Security Clearances for Company Members

**Security Clearance Pathways**:

**Clearance Levels**:
- **Confidential**: Basic clearance for general operations
- **Secret**: Intermediate clearance for sensitive operations
- **Top Secret**: High-level clearance for classified operations
- **SCI (Sensitive Compartmented Information)**: Highest clearance for intelligence operations

**Clearance Process** (9-12 months typical):
1. **Conditional Job Offer**: Initiation of clearance process
2. **SF-86 Form**: Detailed personal information, employment history, foreign contacts
3. **Background Investigation**: Thorough investigation (loyalty, trustworthiness, reliability)
4. **Polygraph Examination**: Some agencies require polygraph (intelligence agencies)
5. **Adjudication**: Final determination of eligibility

**Pathways for Company Members**:
1. **Direct Employment**: Company members apply for clearances through company
2. **Government Contracts**: Clearances required for government contract work
3. **Intelligence Agency Training**: Clearances obtained through civilian leadership training
4. **Congressional Support**: House members can facilitate clearance processes

**Benefits**:
- Access to classified information and operations
- Eligibility for government contracts requiring clearances
- Enhanced credibility and trust
- Career advancement opportunities
- Competitive advantage in security/defense market

**Value**: Security clearances are valuable assets (average compensation for cleared professionals: $119K+ in 2025)
