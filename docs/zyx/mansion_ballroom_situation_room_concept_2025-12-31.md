## Executive Summary

A **luxury event venue + mission-driven command center** that combines:
- **Physical Space**: Mansion with ballroom (extremely high ceilings), Situation Room capabilities
- **Mission**: Save lives, heal the world through real-time coordination
- **Technology**: Grain OS powering real-time event synchronization
- **Culture**: High-intensity, fast-paced, yet controlled, with love and bhakti (devotion)
- **Scale**: Up to 100 people coordinating in real-time, volatile but non-abusive

**Business Model**: Dual-purpose space (luxury events + mission operations) with technology platform

**Revenue Streams**:
1. Luxury venue rental (weddings, cotillions, corporate events) - $10K-$50K+ per event
2. Mission-driven operations (crisis response, humanitarian coordination) - grants, contracts
3. Technology platform licensing (Grain OS coordination system) - SaaS model
4. Transformative experiences (ceremonial, coming-of-age, gift-giving events)
