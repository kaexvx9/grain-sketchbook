## What shifts when angels sit on both sides of the table

Angels and micro-funds already buy **services** at multiples of what they spend on **software licenses**: analyst time, lawyer hours, bookkeepers, ESG narrative shops, “platform diligence” retainers, and **operator hours** inside portfolio companies. Bek’s frame is simple: **sell the closed loop they want**, price it like **professional work**, report it like **a product** (dashboard + weekly note + quarterly partner review). Every model upgrade that automates a slice of your delivery **widens margin**; the **price to the buyer** stays anchored to **value of the outcome**, not to API commodity.

**Copilot path (near):** you sit beside their **principal** or **platform associate**; they own the relationship to the founder; you carry **risk off their calendar** (research, architecture memo, security narrative).

**Autopilot path (far, earned):** they buy **“books closed”** equivalents—**defined artifacts** on a calendar: *Portfolio transparency pack v1 delivered in 21 days. Materials-passport schema for three SKUs. Sponsor-disclosure policy for one creator network.* The buyer signs for **the artifact and the cadence**; your stack makes delivery **cheaper every quarter** as playbooks compound.

The Elon-shaped hardware horizon **still waits** behind a **service-rich PBC** that **profits**; this document simply names that **the fastest margin** may be **white-glove to capital**, not only **self-serve to creators**.

## Premium deliverables catalog (sell these as SKUs)

Each row is a **named package** with a **fixed boundary**, a **visible price**, and a **receipt artifact** (PDF + repo link + signed log where applicable). Mix retainers with **milestone bonuses** the way ColdIQ scales trust.

|-----|-------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Angel Diligence Room — 14-day** | Lead angel or syndicate captain | Source-linked memo, risk register, **one** technical walk (Rye/Basin/Vantage or Grain OS ledger slice), **go / no-go** one-pager | Buys **judgement compression**; priced as **work**, not as “a login” |
| **Portfolio Transparency Sprint — 30-day** | Fund platform team | Visible sponsorship / disclosure **policy kit**, analytics receipt template, **one** integration story for a single portfolio brand | **Autopilot-shaped**: an outcome bundle their founders can adopt |

| **PBC & Purpose Narrative — 10-day** | Mission lawyer + founder pair | Charter-aligned **public benefit** language, investor FAQ, **Silken Ground** rubric appendix | **Governance as deliverable**—hours + craft, not a Notion template alone |

Start with **two SKUs maximum** in market at once. **Raise prices** by refusing the “cheap discovery call” version: every engagement ships a **dated artifact** with your name on it.

## Concentric circles (services-first)

1. **Inner ring — angels and funds as clients:** a short list (Stray Dog–class, Veg Capital–class, Beyond Impact–class, GFI-adjacent intros) receives **one** flagship SKU first. Their **LP letters** get cleaner paragraphs; their **founders** get faster answers; **you** get **cash and references** that do not depend on a single product roadmap bet.
2. **Second ring — portfolio companies:** services engagements **sponsored or introduced** by the angel client become **repeatable playbooks** (the ColdIQ “markdown per task” bank).
3. **Third ring — regional operators and co-ops:** same playbooks, **lower touch**, **software-assisted** delivery—margin expands.
4. **Fourth ring — civic and open infrastructure:** grants, municipal pilots, OSS **sustainability**—fed by **service profits**, not by hope.

Software is still the **spine**; it is simply **bundled inside** the **statement of work** until self-serve maturity earns its own SKU.

## Pricing and proof (what “premium” means here)

- **Setup fee** for any sprint that touches **keys**, **repos**, or **ledger** integrations.  
- **Monthly retainer** tied to **one outcome metric** (artifacts delivered, portfolio hours, passport pages live).  
- **Performance bonus** when a defined threshold clears (e.g., **second** paid referral from the same angel table).  
- **Artifacts:** Loom for anything with a cursor; **Markdown runbook** per repeatable task; **dated decision log** per client; **failed attempts file** (ColdIQ gold).  
- **Public proof without exposing client secrets:** case-study blurbs with **numbers** (“14-day room delivered: 37 primary sources, 4 architectural risks named, 1 ship decision”).

## Ninety-day current (services-anchored)

1. **Pick SKU 1** (Diligence Room or Transparency Sprint) and publish a **single-page scope + price**.  
2. **Land two paying angels or one fund platform** at a **retainer floor** you would still want in year three.  
3. **Bank artifacts** until three look **indistinguishable from a product** when packaged.  
4. **Only then** productize: automate the **fifth** repeat into a **checklisted** web flow; keep the **first four** human-led and **high margin**.  
5. **Parallel:** keep the software wedge from the sibling strategy doc **warming** in the repo—services revenue **buys** the engineering weeks.

## Silken Ground rubric (applied to invoices)

Does the engagement **nourish** the buyer’s mission (clearer, safer, faster)?  
Does revenue **circulate** back into open artifacts and local pilots where promised?  
Does every sprint **return what it takes** (data handed back, keys rotated, founders unblocked)?

If yes, the **rail and canola** chapters in your longer materials become **credible sequels** funded by **service margin**, not by a premature cap table bet.
