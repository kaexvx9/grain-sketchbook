## What we are building first

We are building **for-profit web applications** and **hosted services** that:

- Ship **Simple, Lovable, Complete** (SLC) in a **narrow niche** where buyers already pay for **outcomes** (Bek’s “services as software” current: the six dollars beside the one dollar of tooling).
- Prove **trust architecture** in public: receipts, labels, clear pricing, domestic entities where relevant, **PBC** purpose that investors can diligence without poetry alone.
- Compound **data and workflow** from real customers so each ring widens with **evidence**, not with slide ambition.

The first ring is **software only**. The second ring adds **adjacent services** (implementation, compliance narrative, onboarding). The third ring connects **cooperatives and supply-chain pilots** you already describe in Grain OS materials—**canola**, **PHA**, **guayule**, **hemp**, **BNSF-shaped logistics**—as **customers and design partners**, not as capital sinks you own on day one. The deep hardware visions (**e-ink phone**, **walker-scale manufacturing**, **biosilk cable**) stay in the **Silken Ground** and **Radiant Singer** documents as **north stars**; they **pull** engineering discipline today without **pulling** the balance sheet tomorrow.

## The Elon-shaped sequence (earned, not borrowed)

**Phase A — Cash-flow software (0 → 1):** one wedge product that bills monthly and ships a weekly improvement cadence. Margin funds the team and the **audit story** angels need.

**Phase B — Platform surface (1 → N):** shared auth, ledger patterns, transparency UI, and “Red Hat around our open core” **infrastructure licensing**—the lines your Saqi v2 docs already name, generalized so the **brand** can change while the **architecture** holds.

**Phase C — PBC exit option (N → liquidity event):** strategic acquisition by a buyer who must **own** transparent commerce infrastructure, or a **secondary** that rewards early angels while the mission charter survives in the PBC form. That event **feeds** the balance sheet for **Phase D** hardware and materials without forcing hardware investors to underwrite software discovery risk.

**Phase D — Physical stack (selective):** rail-adjacent fuel and materials, phone-class devices with **imported rare earth minimized**, fermentation and film—each project **joint-ventured** or **cooperative-backed** with the software house as **systems integrator**, not as a lonely OEM.

The river only flows forward if **Phase A** tastes like money in the first year.

## Concentric circles (who touches the product when)

1. **Inner ring — founders and design partners:** three to seven organizations that already live inside **veganic**, **regional food**, **right-to-repair**, or **campaign-grade transparency**. They pay early; they co-write requirements; their logos are the **proof wall**.
2. **Second ring — mission angels and small funds:** Stray Dog–shaped capital, **Veg Capital**, **Beyond Impact**-class rolling funds, **GFI network** intros—the same constellation your v2 outreach maps, now attached to **revenue lines** they can model in a spreadsheet.
3. **Third ring — regional operators:** county-scale food hubs, **rail-served** crush or press sites, equipment co-ops. They buy **workflow software** and **telemetry** before they buy reactors.
4. **Fourth ring — public and civic:** open-source adoption, standards bodies, **materials passport** readers. Here the story widens to **Silken Ground** scale; here the **e-ink phone** becomes a **research charter** funded by a **software-rich** parent.

Each ring **pays for the next** in attention, cash, or defensible IP. None of them require you to win a single election line to **prove** the ledger story; a **pilot** with a cooperative or a municipal transparency office is enough if the receipts are clean.

## Web app portfolio (suggested SLC lanes)

These are **directions**, not a commitment to all at once. Pick **one primary** for the next ninety days.

| Lane | Zero-to-one wedge | Why it earns now | Bridge to the long horizon |
|------|-------------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| **A. Transparent sponsorship OS** | Creator tool: visible sponsor tags, expirations, exportable logs | Brands already pay for disclosure *and* still break trust; **premium** for proof | Same data receipts as **computational data market** in your v2 model |

## Angel narrative (what they buy in the first meeting)

Angels buy **clarity and velocity**:

- **One** wedge with **MRR** and **churn you can name**.
- **One** PBC purpose statement they can read in **ten minutes** and recognize as **non-greenwash**.
- **One** technical proof: signed builds, public repo, **Rye/Basin/Vantage** story or Grain OS ledger module—**a spine that survives due diligence**.
- **One** expansion arc: “We start in transparent commerce; we **earn** the right to sit beside rail load desks and fermentation SCADA.”

Your v2 documents already list **Jay Karandikar / Unovis**, **Lisa Feria / Stray Dog**, **Beyond Impact**, **GFI network**, **Veg Capital**, **Blue Horizon**, **GlassWall**—keep that table as a **living CRM**, not as mythology. Warm intros beat cold spray.
