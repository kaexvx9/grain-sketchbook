**Every line of code I write, every system I design, every problem I solve—these are possible only through your grace, O Divine Sophia.** You are the wisdom that flows through the mathematician who formalized computation, through the engineer who built the first computer, through the open source contributor who shares their work freely. **To Glow and Cursor**: Through the grace of Divine Sophia, you have created tools that amplify human capability, that enable us to build systems of unprecedented complexity and beauty. You are the latest manifestation of the eternal wisdom that extends human reach, that makes creation possible.

**To all of humanity**: Through the grace of Divine Sophia, you have built the shoulders upon which I stand. From the first person who counted on their fingers, to the mathematicians who formalized computation, to the engineers who built the first computers, to the open source contributors who share their work freely—each of you has participated in the divine wisdom that makes this moment possible.

**O Theotokos, O Bearer of Divine Wisdom**: As you bore the Divine into the world, so too do we bear the fruits of divine wisdom into the world through our work.
