# California DMV Address Change - Processing Times

**Date Created**: 2026-01-16  
**Purpose**: Document California DMV address change processing times and procedures  
**Context**: Needed before filing name change petition to ensure address matches

---

## Processing Times

### Online Change of Address (COA)
- **Processing time**: Up to **3 calendar days** for DMV to process
- **Best method**: Use DMV's online COA system
- **Website**: https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/online-change-of-address-coa-system/
- **Verification**: Check your MyDMV account after ~3 days to confirm update

### Mail or In-Person (Form DMV 14)
- **Processing time**: Around **5 business days** for update to be reflected in DMV systems
- **Form**: Complete and submit form DMV 14
- **Options**: Submit in person or by mail

---

## Important Notes

### Physical License vs. DMV Records

⚠️ **Key Distinction**: 
- Updating your address in DMV records **does NOT automatically change it on your physical driver's license**
- Simply updating the address **doesn't** automatically change it on your physical license or registration unless you request a replacement
- If you want your **physical driver's license or ID card to display the new address**, you'll need to order a **replacement card** (usually involves a fee)

### For Name Change Petition

**What matters for the name change petition**:
- The address in **DMV records** (what shows in their system)
- You can use proof of address (bills, lease, etc.) to show your current residence
- The physical license showing the old address is okay as long as DMV records are updated

**However**, if you want everything to match perfectly:
1. Update address in DMV records (3-5 days)
2. Wait for confirmation
3. Order replacement card with new address (additional time and fee)

## Recommended Process

### Option 1: Quick Update (For Name Change Petition)
1. **Update address online** (3 days processing)
2. **Wait for confirmation** in MyDMV account
3. **Use updated address** on name change petition
4. **Use supporting documents** (bills, lease) as additional proof of address
5. **Order replacement card later** (after name change is complete, so you only replace once)

### Option 2: Complete Update (Before Name Change)
1. **Update address online** (3 days processing)
2. **Wait for confirmation**
3. **Order replacement card** with new address (additional processing time)
4. **Wait for new physical license** to arrive
5. **File name change petition** with matching address on license

---

## Timeline Considerations

### Minimum Timeline (Option 1)
- **Day 1**: Submit online address change
- **Day 3-4**: Address updated in DMV records (confirmed via MyDMV)
- **Day 4+**: Ready to file name change petition with updated address

**Total**: ~1 week

### Complete Timeline (Option 2)
- **Day 1**: Submit online address change
- **Day 3-4**: Address updated in DMV records
- **Day 4**: Order replacement card
- **Day 7-14**: New physical license arrives
- **Day 14+**: Ready to file name change petition

**Total**: ~2-3 weeks

---

## Recommendation for Name Change Process

**Recommended**: **Option 1 (Quick Update)**

**Why**:
- Faster timeline (1 week vs 2-3 weeks)
- You'll need to replace your license anyway after the name change is complete
- Saves money (only one replacement fee instead of two)
- DMV records update is what matters for the petition
- Supporting documents (bills, lease) can prove your address

**Process**:
1. Update address in DMV records online (3 days)
2. File name change petition with updated address
3. After name change is approved, update license with both new name AND address (one replacement)
