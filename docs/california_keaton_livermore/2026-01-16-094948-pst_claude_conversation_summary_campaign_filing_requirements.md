## TOP-TWO PRIMARY SYSTEM EXPLAINED

California uses Top-Two Nonpartisan Primary:
- All candidates (all parties) appear on same primary ballot
- Top two vote-getters advance to general election
- Even if both are from same party
- No additional filing fee for general election if advancing
