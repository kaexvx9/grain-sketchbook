# 2026 California Governor Election - Filing Requirements

## Campaign Goal

**Primary Objective**: Win at least second place in the June 2026 Democratic primary election to qualify for the Top Two general election in November 2026.

California uses a "Top Two" primary system where the two candidates receiving the most votes in the primary, regardless of party, advance to the general election. The goal is to secure one of these two positions by finishing first or second in the primary.

## Filing Deadlines

### Primary Election (June 2, 2026)

**Signatures-In-Lieu of Filing Fee Period:**
- **Opens**: December 19, 2025
- **Closes**: February 4, 2026
- **Purpose**: Collect signatures to reduce or eliminate filing fee
- **Note**: Signatures collected can also count toward nomination signature requirement

**Nomination Papers Filing Period:**
- **Opens**: February 9, 2026
- **Closes**: March 6, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST
- **Purpose**: File official declaration of candidacy and nomination papers
- **Critical Deadline**: All required documents must be submitted by **March 6, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST**
  - Declaration of Candidacy
  - Nomination papers (65-100 signatures)
  - Filing fee payment ($4,918.58)
  - Income tax returns (5 years, dual copies)
  - Form 700 (Statement of Economic Interests)

### General Election (November 2026)

**Important**: If candidate finishes in Top Two of primary election:
- **No additional filing fee required** for general election
- Top two candidates advance automatically regardless of party affiliation
- Additional campaign finance reporting required
- Nomination papers and forms must be updated for general election
- **Filing Period**: Typically opens in August 2026, closes 88 days before November election

## Filing Fee

### Fee Amount
- **Filing Fee**: **$4,918.58** (2% of Governor's $245,929 annual salary)
- **Payment Method**: Check or money order payable to "Secretary of State"
- **Payment Deadline**: Must be received by March 6, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST

### Fee Waiver Options

**Signatures-In-Lieu (SIL) Requirements:**
- **For Governor (Statewide Office)**: **6,000 valid signatures** required (per California Elections Code § 8106)
- **SIL Period**: December 19, 2025 – February 4, 2026
- **Note**: Signatures collected during SIL period can also count toward nomination signature requirement

## Required Documents and Forms

### Documents Required by March 6, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST:

1. **Declaration of Candidacy**
   - Official form from Secretary of State
   - Must be notarized

2. **Nomination Papers**
   - **65-100 valid signatures** from registered California voters
   - Signatures can come from any registered voter in California (statewide office)
   - Must be collected on official nomination petition forms

3. **Filing Fee Payment**
   - **Amount**: $4,918.58
   - **Method**: Check or money order payable to "Secretary of State"
   - Must be received by deadline

4. **Income Tax Returns** (Governor-specific requirement)
   - **5 most recent tax years** (2021-2025)
   - **Dual copies required**: 
     - 1 unredacted copy (for official review)
     - 1 redacted copy (for public disclosure)
   - Must be submitted by March 6, 2026 deadline

5. **Form 700 - Statement of Economic Interests**
   - Must be filed with Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC)
   - Deadline: March 6, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST
   - Available at: https://www.fppc.ca.gov/

### Campaign Finance Forms:

1. **Form 501 - Candidate Intention Statement**
   - **Critical**: Must file BEFORE soliciting any contributions or making any campaign expenditures
   - File with county registrar or Secretary of State
   - Establishes candidate committee

2. **Form 410 - Statement of Organization**
   - Must be filed within 10 days of receiving $2,000 or more in contributions
   - Establishes campaign committee with FPPC

3. **Form 460 - Campaign Disclosure Statements**
   - Ongoing reporting requirements
   - Filing schedule based on election calendar
   - Must report all contributions and expenditures
