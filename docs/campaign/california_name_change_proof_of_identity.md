## Valid Proof of Identity Documents

### Primary Documents (Usually Required)

1. **Certified Birth Certificate**
   - Shows legal name at birth, date and place of birth, parentage
   - Must be certified copy (not photocopy)
   - Establishes initial identity
   - **Status**: Required for filing name change petition

2. **Driver's License or State ID Card (with photo)**
   - Shows current name, photo, and address
   - Courts often require matching ID
   - Must be current/valid
   - **Status**: Usually required

3. **U.S. Passport (valid or expired)**
   - Shows citizenship, photo ID, and name
   - Excellent proof especially if birth certificate matches
   - Can be expired but must be official
   - **Status**: Strong proof of identity

4. **Social Security Card**
   - Links name in government records
   - Shows legal name in Social Security system
   - Must be official card (not photocopy)
   - **Status**: Often required, especially for updating records after name change

## California-Specific Requirements

### Online Form Preparation: LawHelp Interactive

**Streamlined Option**: California offers an online tool to help prepare name change forms:
- **Website**: LawHelp Interactive (lawhelpinteractive.org)
- **Service**: Interactive interview that helps fill out required forms
- **Supported Types**:
  - Changing one's own name
  - Changing name to match gender identity (adult only)
  - Changing name(s) of one or more children
  - Family group name changes
  - Gender change recognition with name change and new birth certificate
- **Eligibility**: Must be 18 years or older
- **Note**: Program prepares forms but does NOT file them—you still need to print and file at court
- **Exclusions**: Does not help with name changes during/after divorce, adding/removing parent names on birth certificates, or minor gender/name changes

**Benefits**: Streamlines form preparation, ensures correct information, guides through eligibility questions

### For Filing the Name Change Petition

**Required Documents**:
- Name change petition form (county-specific, can be prepared via LawHelp Interactive)
- **Proof of identity** (one or more of the above primary documents)
- Filing fee ($400-$500, varies by county)
- Publication notice (unless change is for gender identity matching)

### After Court Approval

Once you receive the **Decree Changing Name** (court order), you'll need:

- **Certified copies** of the decree (get multiple copies)
- Supporting IDs in your **old name** (to show continuity)
- Supporting IDs in your **new name** (after updating)

## Document Requirements

### Certified vs. Non-Certified

- ✅ **Certified copies** are required (official, stamped/sealed)
- ❌ **Non-certified copies** (photocopies) usually won't work
- **Note**: Get certified copies from issuing agency (vital records office, DMV, etc.)

### Name Mismatch Issues

If your current ID has a **different name** than your birth certificate, you may need:

- Marriage certificate (if name changed due to marriage)
- Previous court order (if name was changed before)
- Divorce decree (if name changed due to divorce)
- Any documents that connect the name changes

---

## What Courts Look For

Agencies and courts typically verify:

- ✅ Photo ID matches person
- ✅ Date of birth matches across documents
- ✅ Signature matches
- ✅ Social Security number matches (if provided)
- ✅ Name continuity (can trace from birth certificate to current name)

**If any are missing**, you may need additional supporting documentation.

## Recommended Documents to Gather

### For Filing the Petition

1. **Certified birth certificate** (primary)
2. **Current driver's license or state ID** (photo ID)
3. **Social Security card** (government records)
4. **U.S. Passport** (if available, strengthens case)

### After Court Approval (For Updating Records)

1. **Certified copies of Decree Changing Name** (get 5-10 copies)
2. **Old driver's license** (to show continuity)
3. **Birth certificate** (original name reference)
4. **Social Security card** (old name, for updating)

## County-Specific Notes

**Important**: Requirements may vary by county in California. Check with your local county court for:

- Exact forms required
- Specific proof of identity requirements
- Filing procedures
- Fee amounts
- Publication requirements

**Recommended**: Contact your county's Superior Court Clerk's Office or Self-Help Center for exact requirements.

## Timeline Considerations

- **Gather documents**: 1-2 weeks (if you need to order certified copies)
- **File petition**: 1 day (once documents are ready)
- **Court processing**: 4-6 weeks (typical)
- **Get certified copies of decree**: 1-2 weeks after approval
- **Update all records**: 2-4 weeks after receiving decree

**Total time**: 6-10 weeks from filing to having all documents updated
