### 2026-01-21-195105-pst
- **AI Video Generation Research Updated** - Optimized for sketchbook-cartoon comic style with audio-driven body language:
  - **New Focus**: Sketchbook-cartoonified comic-book-style videos with audio-driven gestures
  - **Key Requirement**: Mask-wearing characters with body language matching voice inflections and emphasis points
  - Researched tools: HeyGen Comic Style, Synthesia Express-2, D-ID, AI Studios, Research Models (SyncAnimation, VividAnimator, AsynFusion, etc.), Pika Labs
  - **Primary Recommendation**: **HeyGen Comic Style** - dedicated comic style features, good for masked characters, upper-body gestures sync to audio, user-friendly
  - **Alternative Recommendation**: **Research Models** (SyncAnimation, VividAnimator, AsynFusion) - best precision for gesture matching to emphasis points, full-body control, requires technical expertise
  - **Key Finding**: Commercial tools offer moderate gesture matching; research models offer best precision but require development
  - Comparison matrix updated with audio-driven gesture matching scores
