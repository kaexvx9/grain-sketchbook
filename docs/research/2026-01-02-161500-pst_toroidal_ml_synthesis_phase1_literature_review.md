## Domain 1: Toroidal Polar Coordinates & Higher-Dimensional Geometry

### Current Understanding

**Toroidal Coordinates** are a 3D coordinate system where:
- Two coordinates specify position on a torus (ring)
- One coordinate specifies position along the torus radius
- Extensions to 4D+ involve nested tori or hypertori

**Key Questions**:
- Does a linear algebra analog exist for toroidal coordinate spaces?
- How do matrix operations generalize to curved manifolds?
- What are gradient transformations in toroidal spaces?

### Preliminary Findings

**Mathematical Framework**:
- Toroidal coordinates are a type of curvilinear coordinate system
- Linear algebra operations (matrix multiplication, eigenvalues, etc.) are typically defined on flat vector spaces (ℝⁿ)
- Generalizing to curved spaces requires:
  - Differential geometry (manifolds, tangent spaces)
  - Tensor calculus (covariant derivatives, metric tensors)
  - Information geometry (Riemannian metrics on probability manifolds)

**Information Geometry Connection**:
- Information geometry studies probability distributions as manifolds
- Natural gradient methods use Riemannian metrics on parameter spaces
- Toroidal coordinate spaces could potentially be applied to:
  - Parameter spaces with periodic constraints
