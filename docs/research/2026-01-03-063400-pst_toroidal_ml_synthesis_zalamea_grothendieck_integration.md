- Author of comprehensive guides to Grothendieck's work

**Key Works**:
1. **"Grothendieck: Una guía a la obra matemática y filosófica"** (2019)
   - First comprehensive guide to Grothendieck's published work
   - Mathematical and philosophical analysis
   - Available in Spanish (English translations may exist)

2. **"Synthetic Philosophy of Contemporary Mathematics"** (2012)
   - Available in English (MIT Press)
   - Explores unifying concepts in contemporary mathematics
   - Alternative logics, Peirce and Lautman studies

3. **"Grothendieck: A Short Guide to His Mathematical and Philosophical Work (1949–1991)"** (2024)
   - Chapter in "Handbook of the History and Philosophy of Mathematical Practice"
   - Concise survey of Grothendieck's known work
   - Key periods and developments (1951–1991)

4. **"A Unitary Vision of Grothendieck's 40 Main Years (1951–1991): The Models TSK (Topos of Sheaves over Kripke Models)"**
   - Integration of history, phenomenology, and metaphysics through topos theory
   - Unifying vision of Grothendieck's work
