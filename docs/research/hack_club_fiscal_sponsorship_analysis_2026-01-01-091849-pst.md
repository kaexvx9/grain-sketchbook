## Executive Summary

Hack Club Bank (HCB) is a fiscal sponsorship platform operated by Hack Club, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. HCB provides fiscal sponsorship to over 2,500 organizations, enabling them to operate under Hack Club's tax-exempt status and receive tax-deductible donations. The program is primarily designed for teen-led projects (18 or under) and charges a 7% fiscal sponsorship fee on incoming funds.

**Key Findings**:
- **Eligibility**: Primarily for teen-led projects (18 or under), charitable in nature
- **Fee Structure**: 7% of incoming funds (covers all services, no additional fees)
- **Services**: Nonprofit status, financial tools, tax filings, donation processing, support
- **Transparency**: Optional "Transparency Mode" with public transaction ledger
- **Limitations**: Must be teen-led, cannot be Hack Club HQ affiliated projects

**Notable Example**: Ghostty (terminal emulator by Mitchell Hashimoto) became fiscally sponsored by Hack Club in December 2025, demonstrating that established open-source projects can use HCB even if not teen-led (though this may be an exception).

## 1. Hack Club Fiscal Sponsorship Program Overview

### 1.1 What is HCB?

**Hack Club Bank (HCB)** is Hack Club's fiscal sponsorship program that provides:
- **501(c)(3) Nonprofit Status**: Projects operate under Hack Club's tax-exempt status
- **Financial Tools**: Platform for managing funds, issuing debit cards, tracking transactions
- **Tax Filings**: HCB handles all tax filings, including Form 990
- **Donation Processing**: Customizable online donation forms for fundraising
- **Support**: Dedicated support with 24-hour response time on weekdays

**Scale**: HCB fiscally sponsors over 2,500 organizations.

**Open Source**: In March 2025, HCB open-sourced its entire codebase under the AGPL license, promoting transparency and providing a resource for building financial software.

## 2. Application Process and Requirements

### 2.1 Eligibility Requirements

**Primary Requirement**: **Teen-Led Projects**
- Projects must be led by individuals aged **18 or under**
- The teen applicant must be the organization owner
- For applicants under 18, a parent or guardian must co-sign the fiscal sponsorship agreement

**Project Requirements**:
- **Nonprofit Nature**: Projects must be charitable and not involved in political activities
- **Mission Alignment**: Projects must align with Hack Club's mission (empowering young people interested in technology and coding)
- **Not Affiliated**: Hack Club HQ affiliated projects (e.g., Campfire) are ineligible

**Note**: While the primary requirement is teen-led projects, there are exceptions. Ghostty (by Mitchell Hashimoto, an adult) became fiscally sponsored by Hack Club in December 2025, suggesting that established open-source projects may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

### 2.2 Application Process

**Steps**:
1. **Application Form**: Complete the application at [HCB Application](https://hackclub.com/fiscal-sponsorship/apply/)
2. **Onboarding Call**: After submission, schedule a call with HCB staff
3. **Agreement**: Sign the fiscal sponsorship agreement
   - For applicants under 18, a parent or guardian must co-sign
   - Agreement outlines terms and responsibilities of both parties

## 4. Fee Structure

### 4.1 Fiscal Sponsorship Fee

**Fee**: **7% of incoming funds**

**Coverage**: This fee covers:
- Card transactions
- Staff support
- Legal services
- Tax filings
- All administrative costs

**No Additional Fees**:
- No startup fees
- No subscription fees
- No transaction fees (beyond the 7% fiscal sponsorship fee)

### 4.2 Fee Comparison

**Industry Standard**: Fiscal sponsors typically charge 5% to 15% of funds raised. HCB's 7% fee is within the lower-middle range of industry standards.

**Value Proposition**: The 7% fee covers all services (tax filings, legal compliance, financial tools, support), making it a comprehensive package with no hidden costs.

**Example**: For a project receiving $100,000 in donations:
- HCB receives: $7,000 (7%)
- Project receives: $93,000 (93%)
