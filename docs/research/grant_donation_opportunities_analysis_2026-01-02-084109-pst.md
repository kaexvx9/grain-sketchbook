## 4. Tax-Deductible Donation Requirements

### 4.1 IRS Requirements for Tax-Deductible Donations

**501(c)(3) Status Required**:
- **Fiscal Sponsorship**: Donations are tax-deductible through sponsor's 501(c)(3) status
- **Independent 501(c)(3)**: Donations are tax-deductible through organization's own 501(c)(3) status

**Donation Receipt Requirements**:
- **Written Receipt**: Must provide written receipt for donations of $250 or more
- **Donor Information**: Must include donor name, donation amount, date, and description
- **Tax-Exempt Status**: Must indicate tax-exempt status (501(c)(3))
- **Value of Goods/Services**: If goods/services provided, must indicate value

**Record-Keeping Requirements**:
- **Donor Records**: Must maintain records of all donations
- **Financial Records**: Must maintain financial records for tax filings
- **Form 990**: Must file Form 990 (annual information return) with IRS

### 4.2 Fiscal Sponsorship vs. Independent 501(c)(3)

**Fiscal Sponsorship**:
- **Receipts**: Sponsor (Hack Club) handles donation receipts
- **Record-Keeping**: Sponsor handles record-keeping and tax filings
- **Compliance**: Sponsor ensures compliance with IRS requirements

**Independent 501(c)(3)**:
- **Receipts**: Organization handles donation receipts (must comply with IRS requirements)
- **Record-Keeping**: Organization handles record-keeping and tax filings
- **Compliance**: Organization ensures compliance with IRS requirements
