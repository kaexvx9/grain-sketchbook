## Step 3: Technical Architecture Deep Dive

### 3.1 GrainOS Compute Clusters
**Purpose**: Local self-hosted sustainable-energy-powered compute
**Components**:
- RISC-V toroidal architecture (Mantraphone/Mantralaptop foundation)
- Solar/wind/hydro power integration
- Local mesh networking
- Distributed storage (Silo)
- Grainbank integration for local currency

**Farming Applications**:
- Sensor networks (soil moisture, temperature, pH)
- Autonomous robotics coordination
- Crop monitoring and optimization
- Supply chain tracking
- Worker task assignment (Job Guarantee)

**Community Applications**:
- Local internet infrastructure
- Educational platforms (homeschooling support)
- Community governance (Constitution DAO)
- Local business networks
- Arts and culture platforms

### 3.2 farm-ng Amiga Integration
**Design Principles**:
- Self-assemblable (modular components)
- Open-source hardware and software
- No-till compatible (lightweight, precise)
- Autonomous operation
- Swarm coordination

**Functions**:
- Seeding and planting
- Weeding (precision, no herbicides)
- Harvesting (selective, gentle)
- Soil monitoring
- Pollinator support (native plant integration)

**Job Guarantee Integration**:
- Workers learn robotics and automation
- Maintenance and repair jobs
- Programming and optimization
- Swarm coordination management
- Data analysis and optimization

### 3.3 No-Till Ecological Principles
**Helen Atthowe's Methods**:
- Minimal soil disturbance
- Cover crops and mulching
- Compost and biochar integration
- Native plant integration
- Pollinator habitat creation

**Technology Compatibility**:
- Lightweight robots (no compaction)
- Precision operations (minimal disturbance)
- Sensor networks (monitor soil health)
- Data-driven optimization (reduce trial and error)

## Step 6: Political Strategy and Implementation

### 6.1 Bill Structure Recommendations
**Bill 1: Oregon Job Guarantee Act**
- Establishes Job Guarantee program
- Creates $WAVE currency via Grainbank
- Funds program through currency creation
- Sets wage rates ($18-22/hour, regionally adjusted)
- Establishes benefits (healthcare, childcare, retirement)

**Bill 2: Factory Farm Conversion Act**
- Authorizes purchase of non-vegan animal factory farms
- Establishes conversion to vegan permaculture food forests
- Creates Job Guarantee work opportunities
- Sets environmental standards (no-till, organic, native plants)
- Establishes regional optimization (West/East Cascades)

**Bill 3: Traditional Urbanism Housing Act**
- Authorizes traditional urbanism housing communities
- Establishes sustainable materials requirements
- Creates walkable, mixed-use districts
- Sets density and design standards
- Integrates with Job Guarantee program

**Bill 4: GrainOS Technology Integration Act**
